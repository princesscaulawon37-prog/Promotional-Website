The Breadbasket of Mindanao

Bukidnon is a landlocked province in Northern Mindanao, known for its cool highland climate, fertile plateaus, and vast pineapple and sugarcane plantations. It is home to the Bukidnon, Higaonon, Talaandig, Manobo, and other indigenous peoples whose culture and crafts remain vibrant today.

From the cloud-kissed summit of Mt. Kitanglad — a UNESCO Man and Biosphere Reserve — to the thrilling zip-lines of Dahilayan and the sacred weaves of Tagolwanen, Bukidnon offers an authentic highland experience unlike any other in the Philippines.

Top Attractions

Explore the natural wonders and adventure destinations that make Bukidnon extraordinary.

🐄 Ranch

Communal Ranch

Experience life on one of the largest communal cattle ranches in Southeast Asia, sprawled across the cool highlands of Bukidnon.

🎢 Adventure

Dahilayan Adventure Park

Home to Asia's longest dual zip-line and a host of thrilling rides nestled at 3,000 feet above sea level with panoramic mountain views.

🏔️ Natural Park

Mt. Kitanglad Range Natural Park

A UNESCO Man and Biosphere Reserve teeming with rare flora and fauna, including the Philippine Eagle and over 300 orchid species.

💧 Waterfall

Mindamora Falls

A pristine multi-tiered waterfall hidden within the verdant forests of Bukidnon, offering a refreshing escape from the highland heat.

🍍 Agri-Tourism

Del Monte Pineapple Plantation

Tour the world-famous Del Monte pineapple fields — a sea of green stretching to the horizon on the fertile Bukidnon plateau.

🌄 Ridge

Panimahawa Ridge

An iconic vantage point offering sweeping 360° views of Bukidnon's rolling green hills and mountain ranges at sunrise and sunset.

Culture & Heritage

Bukidnon's soul lives in its indigenous traditions, sacred spaces, and living arts.

⛪ Spiritual

Abbey of the Transfiguration

A serene Benedictine monastery perched amid the pine-scented highlands of Bukidnon, welcoming pilgrims and retreatants seeking peace.

🧵 Indigenous Craft

Tagolwanen Women Weavers

The Tagolwanen Women Weavers Association preserves the age-old tradition of back-strap loom weaving, creating intricate textiles that tell Bukidnon's story.

🎨 Art Gallery

Salima Saway Soil Painting Gallery

Discover breathtaking artworks crafted entirely from natural Bukidnon soil by master artist Salima Saway — a unique testament to the land's rich pigments.
